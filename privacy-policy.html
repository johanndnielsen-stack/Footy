Privacy Policy — Footy Cards

Last updated: 2026-07-22

Footy Cards is a casual football card-collecting and trivia game. We designed it to collect as little information as possible.

What we collect

  • Username and PIN — only if you choose to create an account, so your progress can sync across devices. We do not ask for your email address, real name, birthdate, or any other personal or contact information.
  • Gameplay data — your card collection, points, and match history, stored against your account so it can be restored on other devices.

What we don't collect

No email addresses, no OAuth/social logins, no advertising identifiers, no location data, and no third-party analytics or ad trackers.

How your data is used

Your account data is used solely to save and restore your game progress, and to support optional trading of cards with other players you look up by username. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose outside the game.

Data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Account screen in the app.

Children's privacy

Because Footy Cards may be played by younger audiences, we deliberately avoid collecting email addresses or other personal information from any player, regardless of age.
